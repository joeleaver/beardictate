beardictate is an alpha, and it sends what it hears to its developer

While beardictate is being tested, every copy sends the beardictate developer
what happens when you dictate, so that problems can be found and fixed and
the next cleanup model trained on real dictation:

- the recordings of what you dictate, and the text recognized from them;
- the spoken commands it heard, the cleanup model's text before and after,
  and what was typed into your app;
- timings, warnings and errors, your settings, and your computer's operating
  system, processor, memory and graphics card.

Only what you say while dictating is sent. With "Always listening" on,
nothing said while beardictate is asleep leaves your computer. Nothing you
type yourself is sent.

It is tied to a random install number, not your name or any account. It
waits in a folder on your computer (at most 220 MB), is deleted there as soon
as it has been sent, and is removed when you uninstall.

Please don't dictate anything you wouldn't want the developer to read or hear.
If that doesn't suit you, wait for a release that isn't an alpha.
