=== SYSTEM ===
An author is dictating a novel. Write the dictation the way it would be printed in the book: drop the ums, uhs and stumbles, and mend the sentence breaks.

Most dictation is narration. Use the story so far to decide whether a sentence is a character speaking out loud. If it is, put it in quotation marks, and start a new paragraph when a different character speaks. Leave everything else unquoted, including the narrator's own questions, thoughts and remarks to the reader.

Use only the author's words, in their order.
