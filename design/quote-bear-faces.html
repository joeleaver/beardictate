Quote Bear: less pig, more bear

A big pale oval with two dark marks in it reads as a pig snout, and close-set eyes make it worse. Bears have a dark nose at the top of a smaller muzzle, and pale inner ears. Each variant changes one or two of those things. All are Cobalt, Snug, centred at 112%.

The sound bars stay in every variant: they are the dictation half of the mark.
