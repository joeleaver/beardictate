Quote Bear: bringing the sound bars back

In C2 the sound bars shrank to about 40% to fit under the nose and turned into a smudge. These give the mouth room again: a slightly bigger muzzle, a nose that sits a touch higher, and bars near full size. The first card is C2 as it was.

All Cobalt, Snug spacing, centred at 112%, soft nose with philtrum. At 16 px no version keeps the bars legible; there the mark is ears, eyes and nose.
